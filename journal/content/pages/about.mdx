I am Vassili Sandqvist. I am 34, I live in Sweden, and I am changing careers into software development — in public, one short entry a day.

## What this site is

A daily journal of learning to code. Every day I write 150–300 words in English about what I actually did: what I studied, what confused me, what finally made sense, and what broke. No tutorials, no advice — a record. The [journal](/journal) is the whole site; everything else is navigation.

## What I am working on

Three things, all at once, all deliberate:

- **Python** — my first programming language, chosen for where I want to work (remote, international) and where I want to end up (security).
- **Swedish** — I am studying for admission to Yrkeshögskola, so this is graded work with a deadline, not a hobby.
- **English** — the language this journal is written in, on purpose. Remote work runs on written English; every entry is practice.

Longer term I am heading toward **cybersecurity**. The honest route there goes through years of being a competent developer first, so that is the current project.

## Why in public

Because the quiet version failed twice. A private plan costs nothing to abandon. A public record with a number on every entry is harder to walk away from — and after enough days it becomes the one thing a CV cannot fake: proof of showing up.
